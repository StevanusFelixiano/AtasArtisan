Ataste · Motion

Motion across the app

Carries the tab bar’s motion language (1a) to every screen: springs with a little bounce, a squash on press, edges that stretch like dough, light haptics. Motion starts only from a touch or a real state change, and always comes to rest. It has the ship order for every interaction, the shared springs and haptics, and all 22 ranks as live prototypes with their spec, plus a fix for the onboarding ring, which used to turn forever. Boards: Design system · Tab bar · Flow map
