Icons · redrawn
{{ ic.l }}
24pt grid, 2.25pt stroke, round caps and joins, matching the app’s icon set but a touch heavier so they hold up on Cocoa. Home gets an oven chimney and an arched door; History’s arrow winds back. Filled versions cut their details out in the pill colour.
at 24pt
Selected · idleSelected: filled icon, label, stitched pill. Idle: outline icon only, Liquorice 400.
Pressed · idle tabSquash to 108 × 88%, a Cream halo at 13% behind the icon, icon brightens to Cream.
Pressed · selected tabPill squashes with the content and lightens to #FCE3D0, the same rule as pressed buttons.
DisabledOffline with no cached history. Icon at 32%, no press response. A tap shakes it, fires a light haptic and shows a toast saying why.
Badge · countThings on Home waiting on you: an edit request, a bill to pay. The badge rides on the icon.
Badge · on the selected tab, 9+Same badge over the pill; the Cocoa ring keeps it cut out. Counts above 9 read 9+.
Badge · dotSomething on Home changed since you last looked (a group locked, Atas confirmed) but nothing needs you.
Option · your picture on ProfilePutri’s cake-box giraffe replaces the person icon. The one place the giraffe earns a spot in the bar: it’s her picture, not decoration. Try it with the Profile tab tweak.
