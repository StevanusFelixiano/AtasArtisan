# Ataste Support
Last updated 1 October 2026
Need help with Ataste? Write to support@ataste.app and we will reply within two working days.
## Common questions
- How do I sign in? Tap Continue with WhatsApp. WhatsApp opens with a short code already typed; press send. Ataste reads your number from that message and signs you in.
- My code expired. Codes last 10 minutes. Go back and tap Send again for a new one.
- How do I join a friend's group? Open the link your host shared. If it opens in Safari, copy it, then in Ataste tap Paste a join link.
- Who sends the order to Atas Artisan? The host, from their own WhatsApp, using the message Ataste writes.
- How is ongkir split? Equally between everyone who ordered, after the host enters the amount Atas Artisan confirms.
- How do I delete my account? Profile, then Delete account.
