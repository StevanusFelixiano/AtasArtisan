# Ataste Support
Last updated 14 October 2026
Need help with Ataste? Write to bayu.johar25@gmail.com and we will reply within two working days.
## Common questions
- How do I sign in? Tap Sign in with Apple.
- What is the Verified badge? It shows that the WhatsApp number on an account belongs to that person. To get it, go to Profile and tap Verify with WhatsApp: WhatsApp opens with a short code already typed; press send. Ataste reads your number from that message. You need it to host a group order.
- My code expired. Codes last 10 minutes. Tap Send new code for a fresh one.
- I used Ataste before Sign in with Apple. Sign in with Apple, then verify the same WhatsApp number: Ataste offers to continue as your old account, with its groups and history.
- How do I join a friend's group? Open the link your host shared. If it opens in Safari, copy it, then in Ataste tap Paste a join link.
- Who sends the order to Atas Artisan? The host, from their own WhatsApp, using the message Ataste writes.
- How is ongkir split? Equally between everyone who ordered, after the host enters the amount Atas Artisan confirms.
- How do I delete my account? Profile, then Delete account.
